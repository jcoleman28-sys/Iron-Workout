Cladum Strength

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Cladum Strength does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Workout history, sets, routines and custom exercises
  • Bodyweight entries
  • Preferences such as weight units, rest timer duration and automatic rest timer settings

This data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and UserDefaults. It is never sent to us or to any third party. The app has no account system, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Health information

Bodyweight entries are entered manually by you. Cladum Strength does not access Apple Health or HealthKit, and does not read data from any medical device.

Tracking

Cladum Strength does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use advertising identifiers.

Data retention and deletion

All data remains on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual workouts from the History tab
  • Delete individual weigh-ins from the Body Weight screen
  • Remove all workouts or bodyweight history from Settings > Data
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data

Children

Cladum Strength is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new date.
