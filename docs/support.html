Cladum Strength

Support

Do I need an account?

No. Cladum Strength works entirely on your device. There is no sign-in, subscription or cloud account.

Where is my data stored?

Workouts, routines, custom exercises and bodyweight entries are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's SwiftData framework. They are not uploaded anywhere.

How do I back up or move my data?

A future version may add export and import. For now, deleting the app also deletes its data, so keep that in mind before removing it.

How do I delete my data?

  • Delete individual workouts in the History tab.
  • Delete individual weigh-ins in the Body Weight screen.
  • Use Settings > Data > Delete All Workouts or Delete Body Weight History to remove everything.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

How do I change between kilograms and pounds?

Open Settings > Units and choose Kilograms or Pounds. All weights and bodyweight entries are converted automatically.

Why does the rest timer start automatically?

You can turn this off under Settings > Workout > Start Rest Timer Automatically, and change the default duration there too.

The app starts with an empty exercise library.

The first launch installs the built-in exercise library. If it is empty, open Settings > Data > Restore Default Exercises. Custom exercises you create are kept separately.

Can I edit a workout after finishing it?

Yes. Open the workout from History and tap Edit. You can change the name, dates, notes, weights, reps, sets and exercises.
